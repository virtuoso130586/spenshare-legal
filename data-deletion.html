Account & Data Deletion — Spenshare

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Spenshare account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Send an email to virtuoso1986@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in to Spenshare, with the subject line "Spenshare Account Deletion Request".

Please include:

  • The name shown on your Spenshare account
  • The email address linked to your Google Sign-In

What gets deleted

Once your request is verified, we will delete:

  • Your account profile (name, email address, account identifier)
  • Your push notification tokens
  • Your personal association with any trips (your name/entries are removed from shared trips; other members' own data in a shared trip is retained, since it belongs to the trip as a whole and to them)

Timeframe

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification.

Data not covered

Aggregated, anonymized data that no longer identifies you, and records we are required to keep for legal or security purposes (such as fraud-prevention logs), are not deleted.

For questions about this process, see our Privacy Policy or contact virtuoso1986@gmail.com.
