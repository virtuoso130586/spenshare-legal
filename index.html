Privacy Policy for Spenshare

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Spenshare ("the app", "we", "us") helps groups of friends split shared expenses on trips. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and a unique account identifier from that provider. We use this to identify you within a trip and to let you securely access your trips from any device.
  • Trip and expense data. Trip names, join codes, member lists, individual expense entries (amount, description, who paid, who owes what), comments on expenses, and settlement records that you and your trip's other members enter into the app.
  • Photos you choose to scan. If you use the receipt-scanning feature, the photo you capture is processed to extract line items and amounts. It is not stored longer than needed to complete that extraction.
  • Push notification token. A device token used to deliver notifications (e.g. a new expense was added, you were promoted to owner) via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

How we use this information

  • To operate the core function of the app: tracking, splitting, and settling shared expenses between the members of a trip.
  • To show you which trips and expenses you're part of, and to keep that data in sync across your devices.
  • To send you notifications about activity relevant to your trips.
  • To let trip owners approve or manage who joins a trip.

Who can see your data

Trip and expense data you enter is visible to the other members of that specific trip, that is the app's core purpose. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions), which processes data on our behalf under its own security and privacy commitments.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account and trips remain active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Spenshare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
